### intro
The Great Lakes contain a fifth of Earth's available freshwater and as a result, the climate of this region is unlike any other on the planet. Previous studies have demonstrated that, consistent with trends in air and ocean temperatures, lake temperatures--including those of the Great Lakes, are also increasing globally. The rate and extent of warming in the Great Lakes varies considerably from lake to lake and even _within_ each lake, partly due to the heterogeneity of characteristics within lakes (bathymetry, latitude, fetch, shoreline shape, etc.). Ice cover in the Great Lakes has been recorded for over five decades with information from high resolution, satellite-based instruments.
